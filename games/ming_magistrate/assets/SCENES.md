## county-atlas.png

Use case: historical-scene. Original Chinese ink wash painting for a Ming dynasty magistrate game, Zhejiang inland county circa 1561. Traditional hand brushed carbon ink with subtle grey-green washes, ivory xuan paper fibers, atmospheric and richly observed, matching a refined Chinese river town ink landscape. No geometric vector look, no modern objects, no frames, no lettering, no calligraphy, no seals, no UI, no watermark. Wide landscape 3:2. A complete bird's-eye illustrated county map on one sheet, all land features inside the image with a generous paper margin. A small walled county town and its magistrate hall at 57% across and 38% down; countryside fields and farmhouse cluster at 21% across and 39% down; lively market lanes at 43% across and 56% down; grain storehouses by the river at 74% across and 59% down; a curved river embankment and wooden ferry at 29% across and 78% down. River winds through lower part, distant mountains at top. Five distinct places, recognizable architectural landmarks. Painterly irregular mountains, tiled roofs and brush foliage, not a clean schematic. No labels whatsoever.

## river-rain.png

Use case: historical-scene. Original hand-painted Chinese ink wash illustration for a Ming magistrate game, inland Zhejiang 1561. Finely observed brush drawing, grey-green washes, charcoal ink, quiet ivory xuan paper fibers, restrained pale earth colors, atmospheric authentic rural Jiangnan. Match a detailed traditional landscape painting rather than geometric vectors, photography or cartoon. Landscape 3:2 image. No lettering, no calligraphy, no seals, no UI, no watermark, no modern objects. Summer monsoon on a Zhejiang earthen river embankment. Three small laborers in straw rain capes placing woven earth baskets against a leaking bank, a county runner observing with a bamboo staff, clustered near the central x=50%, y=55%. Swollen river, a grain boat moored safely beyond, bending reeds, grey rain and ink mist. Show practical flood defense and human effort, no catastrophically broken dam, no drowning. Compact subject group for vertical cropping, restrained grey-green wash.
