## farmland-autumn.png

Use case: historical-scene. Original hand-painted Chinese ink wash illustration for a Ming magistrate game, inland Zhejiang 1561. Finely observed brush drawing, grey-green washes, charcoal ink, quiet ivory xuan paper fibers, restrained pale earth colors, atmospheric authentic rural Jiangnan. Match a detailed traditional landscape painting rather than geometric vectors, photography or cartoon. Landscape 3:2 image. No lettering, no calligraphy, no seals, no UI, no watermark, no modern objects. Autumn field inspection on a narrow paddy ridge. Two complete small human figures, a modest Ming county clerk carrying a rolled measuring cord and a farmer holding cut rice, stand close together within the central 35% of the image, both heads and hands fully inside x=35–60%, y=35–75%. Ochre rice stubble, pale harvested fields, low whitewashed village houses, distant mist mountains. Boundary stones and a bamboo measuring pole make this scene recognizably about surveying, with no written marks. Keep the human group compact enough to remain visible in a narrow vertical crop. Warm but very muted autumn wash, a different viewpoint from an ordinary spring paddy scene.
